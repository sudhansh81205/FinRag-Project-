SPEAKING SCRIPT
We tested the original idea that source structure and retrieval affect grounded
financial QA. We froze the questions and original-source references, used the PRD's
1% numerical tolerance with unit/period/source checks, and compared dense retrieval
against BM25/RRF60 under identical controls. Our numeric reference agreement was
83.1%, with false refusals and semantic traps kept in the denominator. Lexical
fusion did not improve retrieval on this subset. We therefore report a bounded
development result and documented limitations, not a claimed full A-G success.

DEFEND THE PROPOSAL HONESTLY
The PRD defines the research objective and matched controls. This study follows
those controls for one component and validates the actual application. It does
not prove the entire proposal complete. Positive retrieval improvement was a
hypothesis, not a result we were entitled to assume. Full accepted corpora,
source-equivalent official benchmark adapters, independent review and the remaining
experiments are still required before making the broader original claims.
