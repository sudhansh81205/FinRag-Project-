What risks does Oracle Corporation describe in its FY2024 annual report?

Actual answer

### Direct Answer:
Oracle Corporation identifies several risks, including being subject to local securities regulations and inability to exert full control over Oracle Financial Services Software Limited and Oracle Corporation Japan. Additionally, the company is a target for computer hackers, cyber threats, and other bad actors due to the large amounts of data it stores and processes.
