## Slide 2: Problem Statement and Motivation
- **The Financial QA Challenge**:
  - SEC Form 10-K filings contain complex narratives (Item 1A Risk Factors, Item 7 MD&A), multi-year tables and footnotes (Item 8), and stock performance charts (Item 5).
  - Standard RAG splits tables arbitrarily, losing header/unit associations, and LLMs hallucinate calculations without grounded citations.
- **The Grounding Imperative**:
  - Plausible numbers are dangerous: arithmetic performed on the wrong company, period, or unit is an invalid answer (e.g., attributing Apple FY2021 margin to Microsoft FY2022).
- **Core Research Question**:
  - *With corpus, embedder, answer model, prompt, calculator, and 5 final passages fixed, how do section-aware chunks, self-describing cells, hybrid retrieval, hierarchical reranking, routing, and chart descriptions change recall, answer quality, faithfulness, and latency versus standard dense RAG?*

## Slide 3: Related Work & Theoretical Foundation
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**:
  - Dense RAG paradigms (Lewis et al., 2020); limitations of standard chunking on tabular financial structures.
- **Financial Benchmarks**:
  - Hybrid text-table reasoning in FinQA (Chen et al., 2021) and TAT-QA (Zhu et al., 2021); open-domain evaluation in FinanceBench (Islam et al., 2023).
- **Dense Representation & Cross-Encoding**:
  - Pretrained bi-encoders (`BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5`, 768-D L2-normalized) with exact FAISS `IndexFlatIP`; joint cross-attention with `bge-reranker-base`.
- **Hybrid Retrieval & Information Fusion**:
  - Okapi BM25 (Robertson & Zaragoza, 2009) for exact identifier matching; Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF \(k=60\), Cormack et al., 2009) for rank-based merge.
- **Evaluation & Faithfulness**:
  - RAGAS (Es et al., 2023); lexical overlap diagnostics as verifiable local baselines.

## Slide 4: System Architecture & Methods (Variants A–G)
- **Controlled Cumulative Progression (A–G)**:
  - **A**: Dense baseline (fixed-overlap text chunks + flattened Markdown tables)
  - **B**: Section-aware narrative chunks (Item 1A, Item 7) + flattened tables
  - **C**: Self-describing cell facts + parent table expansion (dense only)
  - **D**: Hybrid fusion (Dense + BM25 with RRF \(k=60\))
  - **E**: Hierarchical candidate restriction + `bge-reranker-base` cross-encoder
  - **F**: Bounded keyword/short-model routing + escalation across tiers
  - **G**: Independently approved source-linked chart descriptions
- **Knowledge Representation & Reasoning (KR&R)**:
  - Typed facts (`issuer`, `accession`, `metric`, `period`, `unit`, `scale`, `source_locator`).
  - Strict pre-top-k eligibility filtering; deterministic refusal on missing premises.
- **Authoritative Decimal Arithmetic**:
  - Cited Decimal calculation trees; no arbitrary code execution; operands restricted strictly to included context.
