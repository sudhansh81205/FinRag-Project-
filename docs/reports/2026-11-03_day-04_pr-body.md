the notebook. This change adds `python scripts/parse_filing.py`, producing 316
source-linked narrative records from Items 1A, 7 and 8 and three full statement

copies from narrative evidence, preserves comparative headers and declared unit
exceptions, and rejects source/checksum/layout mismatches. A small compressed
browser snapshot of the canonical SEC filing enables offline reproduction;
its capture method and snapshot-byte checksum are explicitly documented.
