- Why is the total-revenue predicate needed? Correct arithmetic over a product-sales cell can still answer the wrong financial question. The original total row and requested period are separate premises.
- Why does a verified model hash not prove financial accuracy? It proves which weights executed; original-source interpretation and the supported calculation still require evidence.

- Why can’t the app enable charts after checking image bytes alone? Byte identity does not establish caption truth or independent review. Questions and generated captions need distinct decisions.
