## 3. Course concepts and viva questions

1. **Knowledge Representation & Reasoning (KR&R)**:
   - *Question*: Why must company and period filters be applied before top-k retrieval rather than as post-retrieval filters?
   - *Answer*: Post-retrieval filtering on a global top-k can discard all candidates for a specific company or comparative period if another entity has higher dense similarity scores. Pre-top-k filtering enforces the typed KR premise that only facts belonging to the requested issuer and period are eligible to participate in inference.

2. **Deep Learning & Transformer Semantics**:
   - *Question*: What happens when the cross-encoder in Variant E or F fails or produces non-finite scores?
   - *Answer*: The system raises a typed `RetrievalStageError` and halts generation with `service_error`. Falling back to dense-only scores or mock data would invalidate the ablation study by claiming a successful transformer cross-encoder measurement where none occurred.

3. **Safe Inference in Financial QA**:
   - *Question*: Why does the calculator refuse to compute margin growth if one of the operands is excluded by the context token budget?
   - *Answer*: Grounded financial QA requires every operand to be inspectable in the final evidence. Generating a calculation using uncited external constants or hallucinated inputs violates safety and provenance guarantees.
