**Viva Topic**: *Why must gold answers be strictly excluded from the evaluation input?*  
In financial RAG, exposing gold answers or arithmetic programs in prompt context creates catastrophic data leakage, turning an evaluation of retrieval and reasoning into trivial memorization or copy-pasting. By enforcing an immutable `BenchmarkCase.to_eval_input()` barrier, the retriever and LLM only receive the filing question and candidate context, ensuring that accuracy metrics (1% relative tolerance) reflect genuine reasoning over grounded evidence.
