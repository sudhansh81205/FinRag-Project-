## Explain it in the viva

1. **BGE turns text into vectors.** Similar meanings tend to have similar directions.
   The pinned revision and query instruction make builds reproducible; changing
   the model/preprocessing forces a rebuild, because coordinates are not portable.
2. **Normalization makes inner product equal cosine.** For unit vectors u and v,
   cosine is u dot v. FAISS exact search checks every eligible vector; there is no
   training, quantization or approximation error from IVF-PQ.
3. **Rows are not source IDs.** FAISS returns numeric positions. Ordered sidecars
   bind those positions to stable evidence IDs, full metadata and source locators.
   A swapped row must fail even if every file remains valid JSON.
4. **A cell is a self-contained fact.** “365,817” alone is ambiguous; its record
   retains Apple, Total net sales, the 2021 fiscal period, USD-million scale, table
   parent and original coordinates. It normalizes to USD 365,817,000,000.
5. **Filtering after top-k loses eligible answers.** Select the requested company
   and period first, then rank within that set. An unindexed company has no answer.
6. **Reingestion replaces one filing.** A new immutable generation becomes active
   only after validation. Removed rows disappear from active search; other filings
   remain. Checksums catch corruption; the independent source audit checks meaning.
7. **Retrieval is not financial reasoning.** Similarity does not prove a fact or
   justify arithmetic. Typed comparability rules, cited operands and the restricted
   calculator belong to the reasoning stage. BM25/RRF is not A*, minimax or an agent.
