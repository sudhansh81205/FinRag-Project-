FinRAG benchmark results
=========================

Measured November 18, 2026 on application revision 4d168800.
Study type: PRD-aligned development validation.

Application checks
------------------
90 local requests: 72 numerical, 12 adversarial and 6 narrative.
Coverage: 18 indexed SEC Form 10-K filings from 10 companies.

Numerical agreement: 59 of 71 labelled requests (83.10%).
95% filing-cluster interval: 66.67% to 95.83%.
A pass needed a value within 1% relative error, correct units and
value period, the selected filing, and all required cited operands.
The remaining numerical request correctly refused to answer Oracle
gross margin due to missing aggregate evidence. None of the 59
successful labelled responses returned an incorrect numeric value.
The 12 other labelled requests are retained as failures.

Strict adversarial outcomes: 7 of 12 passed (58.33%). The failures
included a refusal with the wrong status, component revenue answered
with consolidated totals, confusion between relative percent and
percentage points, and an incomplete multi-metric answer.

Narrative: all 6 requests returned answers whose evidence IDs and
filing matched the included context. Claim-level correctness remains
unreviewed. These counts are structural citation checks.
Observed local median / p95 latency: 0.028 / 0.050 s for labelled
numerical requests and 102.997 / 157.759 s for narrative requests.
The numerical path used validated Decimal operations. Model residency
and production load were outside the timing protocol.

Retrieval benchmark
-------------------
71 labelled questions shared the original query, pinned BGE embeddings,
exact FAISS, fixed canonical table cells and the same filing filters.
Hybrid added BM25 and reciprocal rank fusion with rank constant 60.

Metric                           Dense only      Dense + BM25/RRF
Operand nDCG@10                       0.6029                0.5893
Required-operand recall@5             75.35%                75.35%
Required-operand recall@10            90.14%                88.73%
All required operands, top 5          61.97%                61.97%
All required operands, top 10         83.10%                77.46%
MRR@10                                0.5410                0.5308

nDCG gives more credit to a relevant result near the top. Each
required operand earns one discounted gain at its first occurrence.
Equivalent rows for the same operand cannot earn repeated credit.
The supplemental nDCG scores were computed from the original saved
rankings and source labels, with both input digests verified. There
were no new model calls or ranking changes.

Paired nDCG@10 difference (hybrid minus dense): -0.0136.
95% interval: [-0.0598, +0.0383], from 10,000 whole-filing bootstrap
resamples with seed 42. The comparison found no evidence that adding
BM25/RRF improved this retrieval metric on these questions.

PRD and presentation
--------------------
The study covers a retrieval component of the PRD's C-to-D transition
and the supported application's answer checks. The questions are a frozen
development set, including cases used during implementation. Official
FinanceBench, FinQA, TAT-QA and chart benchmark scores remain pending.
The PRD acceptance preflight found 150 FinanceBench, 1,147 FinQA and
1,663 TAT-QA candidate cases. It checked all 28 suite/variant paths:
21 text/table paths were blocked and 7 chart paths awaited independent
review. Zero final runs completed. The benchmark runner does not yet
retrieve from validated source-equivalent official contexts. Accepted
representations, chart execution, RAGAS, the separate Llama comparison
and the independent 50-output audit remain outstanding.

In the viva, show numerical agreement and the dense/hybrid table. Then
explain that the retrieval experiment held the questions, sources and
embeddings fixed. Open a correct answer and a failure from the actual
ledger to show how period, units, and evidence affect scoring. Label
the results as development validation and identify the official runs
and independent reviews still needed to meet the full PRD.
